    Origins and Early Training

    Born on January 21, 1997, in Halle, Germany, to Georgian parents, Ilia Topuria has a brother named Aleksandre. After living in Georgia, the brothers moved to Spain settling in Alicante as teenagers, where they trained Brazilian jiu-jitsu and mixed martial arts at the Climent Club gym, having first practiced Greco-Roman wrestling at school.

    UFC Debut and Rise to Volkanovski

    Topuria joined the UFC and made his promotional debut in October 2020 against Youssef Zalal, winning by unanimous decision. Building momentum with first-round knockouts over Damon Jackson and Ryan Hall, plus wins against Jai Herbert and Bryce Mitchell, he defeated Josh Emmett by unanimous decision in June 2023 to earn his title shot.

    Championship Triumphs and Second Belt

    At UFC 298, Topuria knocked out Alexander Volkanovski in the second round to win his first belt, the featherweight title. He defended it by knocking out Max Holloway in round three, then moved up to lightweight and knocked out Charles Oliveira in the first round at UFC 317 to claim his second belt.
